How this website works ✨

  1. Fill in the details: your friend’s name, your name, a wish message, and the age if you know it (optional).
  2. Pick a style (kids, candy, space, ocean, blossom, neon or dark & gold) and one or more wish methods. They play one after another.
  3. Add photos if you like (up to 3, optional).
  4. Tap Create my link. Everything is packed inside the link itself, so there is no account and no server.
  5. Send the link. Your friend opens it, sees a countdown and a curtain reveal, plays your wish games, and finally gets your message and photos.

Tip: use Preview to try it yourself first. Photos are shrunk to fit inside the link, so keep them to 3 or fewer.
